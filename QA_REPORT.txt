SCOPE
This release addresses five owner-observed defects plus one directly related Exit visibility defect discovered during QA:
1) Exit during Test did not cancel the active measurement lifecycle.
2) Real-World Use ratings could be overly pessimistic because AIM classifications include packet-loss semantics while Velnox intentionally omits packet-loss measurement.
3) Live Diagnostic visual stage jumped backward with interleaved latency probes.
4) Sound Effects could be silent/unreliable on Android/PWA AudioContext lifecycle.
5) Secondary typography/contrast was too subdued for comfortable mobile reading.
6) HTML hidden state for Exit could be overridden by the icon-button display rule in an ordinary browser tab.
