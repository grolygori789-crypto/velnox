RELEASE SCOPE
V1.9.0 adds two isolated product features:
1. Share Result
   - Current result: locally generated branded PNG (1080x1350).
   - Optional exact metrics, Country standing and Worldwide standing.
   - Native device/browser file Share where supported.
   - Download Image fallback where direct file sharing is not supported.
   - Share actions on saved History results.
   - Historical result cards deliberately do not reconstruct Country/Worldwide standing because History does not store the benchmark standing shown at the original measurement time.
2. Benedict Interactive studio identity
   - Studio: Benedict Interactive.
   - Studio location: Bangkok, Thailand.
   - Location is presented as studio identity, not as a registered-office/corporate-registration claim.

PRIVACY / DATA CONTRACT
- Result-card rendering is local Canvas rendering in the browser.
- share.js contains no fetch/XHR/sendBeacon/WebSocket/EventSource transport.
- No raw IP is placed on the card.
- No saved-history dump is placed on the card.
- No detailed diagnostics or precise user location is placed on the card.
- Country/Worldwide and exact metrics are user-controlled share-card options.
- Native Share hands the generated PNG to the browser/OS share interface only after the user chooses Share.
- Legal notices updated coherently to legal version 1.1.0.
